# The FBI (Conclusion)

## OVERVIEW

The FBI search the ground floor of the house, but can't seem to get into the basement.<br>
They realize the door is made of indestructible steel so they assume nobody's behind it.<br>
<br>
"All clear", you faintly hear. "Cross this place off the list".<br>
<br>
You realize that the keypad must have retracted into the door somehow. <br>
*That's another mystery I'll solve when I wake up* you mumble to yourself.<br>
<br>
The granny slowly goes upstairs to make sure her items are still there.<br>
When she comes back down, she sits in the chair next to your bedside table.<br>
"Y'know those FBI people? I blackmailed one of them and made them turn a blind eye on you and I", she calmly says with a subtle smile. "One last thing before you head off to sleep".<br>
<br>
She hands you a piece of paper.<br>
It reads:<br>
<br>
CTS{safety_is_here_congratulations}<br>
<br>
You've completed the HaXOR gauntlet. Congratulations!<br>
