# A Magical Prison

## OVERVIEW

After you awaken from your second hex, the first thing you notice is a grandma smiling in the gallery. The second thing you notice is the FBI's fury. They put you in handcuffs and take you to a magical prison, sort of like an Alcatraz, but every cell is made of magical bars, capable of being wielded if you say the right thing or use a dark magic key.

The head chief of the FBI looks at you dead in the eye. "You weren't supposed to get this far. This is your punishment." he barks at you.
