# A Basic Mission

## OVERVIEW

It's a great day to be a hacker. Or should I say... HaXOR?

Today's the day you've been training for. You've decided to hack into the pentagon.

The pentagon's defense login portal is oddly simple. Looks like you're going to have a particularly easy day.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                   LOGIN:

XOR the follow 2 **binary** numbers.

1001101 & 1101010

Give your answer in base 10.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

> Points awarded: 5

## THE FLAG

Spoilers below!

<details>
<summary>The flag is:</summary>
<code>CTS{39}</code>
</details>

## INTENDED SOLUTION

<details>
<summary>
The solution is:
</summary>
Pop both binary values into an XOR calculator and ask it to spit out a decimal number (base 10) instead of a binary number.
</details>
