# Simple Math - Intro to CTF Flags

## OVERVIEW

#### Skip to the bolded text if you know how to submit flags.
Otherwise, read as normal.

When you do a CTF, the answer must include the maker's prefix (ours is CTS) and a set of curly brackets { } wrapped around your answer. The curly brackets are next to the P key on a keyboard. For example, if your answer is 24, you will submit CTS{24}

#### For this challenge, you'll be doing simple math to prove you know how flags work.
What's 8 + 7?

Flag is in the format CTS{answer}

> Points Awarded: 1

## THE FLAG

Spoilers below!

<details>
<summary>The flag is:</summary>
<code>CTS{15}</code>
</details>

## INTENDED SOLUTION

<details>
<summary>
The solution is:
</summary>
8 + 7 is 15. Wrap your answer in curly brackets.
15 -> {15}

Remember to attach the maker's prefix.
{15} -> CTS{15}
</details>
